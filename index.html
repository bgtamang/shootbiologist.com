About Me

Pioneering Crop Science
for a Sustainable Future

With a robust foundation in crop sciences, my role as a Research Scientist at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign centers on advancements in agricultural sciences. Our team's work on the RIPE project contributes significantly to global change and photosynthesis research, pushing the boundaries of crop performance optimization.

Leveraging expertise in plant physiology and molecular biology, I am committed to developing tools that integrate high throughput crop physiology and biotechnology. These efforts are crucial in addressing the pressing challenges of food security and sustainability, reflecting my dedication to scientific excellence and passion for creating positive impact on global agriculture.

Research Interests

  • Photosynthesis optimization in crop plants
  • High-throughput phenotyping technologies
  • Plant stress physiology and adaptation
  • Sustainable agriculture and food security
  • Integration of biotechnology in crop improvement
Research Focus

Transforming Agriculture
Through Innovation

My research focuses on understanding and improving crop productivity through advanced physiological and molecular approaches.

🌱

Canopy Architecture & Photosynthesis

Developed 200+ soybean backcrossed lines and 20 transgenic lines with altered canopy architecture using CRISPR gene editing to optimize light interception and photosynthetic efficiency.

🛰️

High-Throughput Phenotyping

Implementing cutting-edge remote (UAV and Satellite) and proximal sensing technologies (LiDAR, hyperspectral) for rapid assessment of plant traits and stress responses.

💧

Plant Water Relations

Characterizing transpiration dynamics, including nighttime water use and circadian control, to identify traits for improved water use efficiency in crops.

🧬

Stress Physiology

Investigating transcriptomic and hormonal responses to flooding and drought stress in soybean, identifying key genes and pathways for stress tolerance.

🔬

Genetic Resource Development

Creating mapping populations and near-isogenic lines for genetic dissection of complex traits including leaf shape, canopy architecture, and photosynthetic efficiency.

📊

Field-Based Phenomics

Establishing field-based phenotyping platforms integrating multiple sensors for real-time monitoring of crop performance under varying environmental conditions.
